# Sample dataset: synthetic electricity demand

- **File:** `electricity_demand.csv` (485 data rows, 6 columns)
- **Source:** synthetic, generated by `scripts/generate_fixture.py` with a fixed seed. No real
  or sensitive data; no licensing constraints.
- **Domain:** daily electricity demand for four regions over 120 days from 2025-01-01.
- **Columns:** `record_id`, `date`, `region`, `demand_mwh`, `temperature_c`, `holiday`.
- **Deliberately planted data-quality cases:** 12 missing `temperature_c`, 4 missing
  `demand_mwh`, 3 extreme `demand_mwh` outliers (3.5x), and 5 exact duplicate rows.
- **Regenerate:** `python scripts/generate_fixture.py` (output must be byte-identical; a test
  enforces this).
